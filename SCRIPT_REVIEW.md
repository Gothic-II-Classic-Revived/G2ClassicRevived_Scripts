# Open script review findings

## Quests and world placement

- **Ten NPC spawns lack a destination.** Two Khorinis lurkers use `NW_BIGMILL_FIELD_LURKER`. Eight Valley orcs use seven shaman-room points present in the uncompiled surface and absent from the active Valley. These encounters need restored waypoints or new locations. [Startup spawns](CONTENT/Story/Startup.d)
- **Two Valley callbacks are missing.** The pass and mine entrances both request `ENTERTHROUGHORIGINALPASS`. The Free Mine wheel checks `MC_FM_GATE` before activating `FREEMINEGATE`. Neither set of scripts defines what the entrance should record or when the wheel should operate. [Valley world](../Worlds/ValleyOfMines/OLDWORLD.ZEN)
- **Two interactive objects have unresolved bindings.** One Valley alchemy bench requests the nonexistent flask `ITMIFLASK` and gives `POTIONALCHEMY_S1` as its callback base. Neighboring benches use `ITMI_FLASK` and `POTIONALCHEMY`. In Khorinis, a bookshelf requests `Use_BookstandRevived_SPECIAL1`. Its intended text or reward is unknown. [Valley world](../Worlds/ValleyOfMines/OLDWORLD.ZEN) · [Khorinis world](../Worlds/NewWorld/NewWorld.zen)
- **Bounties have three blockers before release.** Mist’s spawn point `NW_FARM3_VALLEY_019` is absent from active Khorinis. Reading a test letter outside Khorinis can consume its one-time spawn flag. Andre’s reward callback leaves a dead bounty eligible for another payout. [Letters](CONTENT/Items/REVIVED/IT_MISSION.d) · [Andre’s payout](CONTENT/Story/Dialoge/DIA_PAL_311_Andre.d)

## NPC routines

- **Rengaru, Baltram, and Fernando can be jailed together at one waypoint.** Each arrest assigns both daily shifts to `NW_CITY_HABOUR_KASERN_RENGARU`. The closest standing marker belongs to Boltan outside the cell. Separate positions inside the cell, or a choice of other cells for these prisoners, are still needed. [Rengaru](CONTENT/Story/NPC/BDT_492_Rengaru.d) · [Baltram](CONTENT/Story/NPC/VLK_410_Baltram.d) · [Fernando](CONTENT/Story/NPC/VLK_405_Fernando.d)
- **Cedric and knight 215 practice swordplay at the same point from 07:08 to 19:00.** Both repeatedly walk back to `NW_CITY_TRAIN_01`. Knight 209 already uses `NW_CITY_TRAIN_02`, and Girion stands close to the other possible spot. A separate position with room for the sword swings still needs to be chosen. [Cedric](CONTENT/Story/NPC/PAL_216_Cedric.d) · [Knight 215](CONTENT/Story/NPC/PAL_215_Ritter.d)
- **Gorn and Milten share one conversation waypoint after Gorn’s release.** Both are scheduled at `OCC_CHAPEL_LEFT_ROOM` from 08:00 to 23:00. The room lacks a pair of smalltalk markers. The nearest other waypoint is 5.43 m away, beyond the 5 m speech range. Their standing positions in the chapel need an in-game check. [Gorn](CONTENT/Story/NPC/PC_Fighter_OW.d) · [Milten](CONTENT/Story/NPC/PC_Mage_OW.d)
- **Two shrine visitors keep their places after Vatras leaves.** Citizen 499 and Rosi, if sent to the city, can still use the standing markers in front of the empty preaching spot. The other listeners change routines. Whether these two should stay at the shrine needs an in-game check. [Citizen 499](CONTENT/Story/NPC/VLK_499_Buerger.d) · [Rosi](CONTENT/Story/NPC/BAU_936_Rosi.d)
- **Crowded bandit camps need a conversation check.** The engine returns one candidate per AI state. If it picks a guard or sitter for `ZS_Bandit`, the speaker can overlook another bandit who is ready to talk. Whether the placed guards and conversation markers cause this in practice is unverified. [Partner selection](CONTENT/AI/Human/B_Human/B_Say_Smalltalk.d)

## Effects and textures

- **Control’s charging effect requests a missing child.** The G1 script also comments out the caster’s head effect, even though the bridge requests it and the particle exists. G2A supplies no active replacement. The open choice is whether charging should show an effect on the caster’s head. [Control bridge and child definition](system/VISUALFX/VisualFxInst.d)
- **Transformation and the Stone of Wisdom request a missing animated texture.** `WAVEOFINSANITY_A0.TGA` is used by the transformation particles and `CS_FOKUS2`. No matching frames turned up in the mod or the clean G1, G2 Classic, and G2A archives. The intended replacement artwork is unknown. [Transformation particles](system/PFX/PfxInstMagic.d) · [Stone of Wisdom particle](system/PFX/PfxInst.d)
- **Fly clouds in Khorinis and the Valley lack their texture.** The GMDK contains the exact source at `H:\Gothic\_GMDK\BaseTextures\Effects\BLOODKAI.TGA`. It still needs to be packaged with the mod’s textures. The alpha mask matters because the particle tints the entire image black. [Fly-cloud particle](system/PFX/PfxInst.d)
- **Several stock particle presets have missing resources and no confirmed consumer in Revived.** The water-outlet and splash presets lack water textures, and one Mass Death preset lacks `MFX_Spell_Star.tga`. The retail G1 and G2 databases also lack the child emitters `ZBLOOD1_SPLAT`, `ZZTEST_EXPL_DEBRIS`, and `CAST9`. G1 version 0.94b provides the blood splat with a texture Revived ships. Its debris effect needs two more child emitters and a ten-frame texture sequence, of which Revived ships one frame. [Engine particles](system/PFX/PfxInstEngine.d) · [Water particles](system/PFX/PfxInst.d) · [Spell and explosion particles](system/PFX/PfxInstMagic.d)

## Older saves

- A fire mage who received Pyrokar’s list before the quest flag was added can still miss Milten’s chapter-four possession report. The flag is set during the one-time handover. [Pyrokar’s handover](CONTENT/Story/Dialoge/DIA_KDF_500_Pyrokar.d) · [Milten’s report](CONTENT/Story/Dialoge/DIA_PC_Mage_NW.d)
- An older save can contain duplicate lighthouse scenery from repeated world entry. Chapter checks recognize an existing cleaned tree. Removal of several same-named roots from a previously affected save still needs in-game verification. [Lighthouse scenery check](CONTENT/Story/B_Content/B_CheckJacksLighthouse.d)
- A player who finished the alchemy bookstands before the journal topic existed and later trained ordinary alchemy may lack both recipe entries. The saved skill flag cannot distinguish that sequence from one that already logged the recipes. Previously recorded tobacco entries can also retain the old ingredients. [Craft status recovery](CONTENT/Story/RevivedHelpers.d)
